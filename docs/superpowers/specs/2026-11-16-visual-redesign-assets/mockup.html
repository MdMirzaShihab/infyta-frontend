Network Infrastructure

Design, installation and maintenance of office networks for businesses in Dhaka.

Get Started Today+880 1318 121558

Overview

Infyta designs, installs and maintains computer networks for offices and businesses in Dhaka. A typical project covers the wired network, Wi-Fi, switches, routers and firewalls, set up so that staff stay connected and business systems are protected. We work on networks of different sizes, from a single small office to larger enterprise networks.

Our engineers work with MikroTik, Cisco and Fortinet equipment, and the team includes engineers and instructors certified in CCNA, MTCNA, MTCRE and MTCSE. We can build a network for a new office, expand one that has outgrown its original design, or review and fix a network that has become slow or unreliable. Once the network is running, we can maintain it and adjust it as your business changes.

What’s Included

  • Network DesignPlanning the layout, equipment and addressing for a new or expanded office network.
  • Structured CablingStructured cabling that connects workstations, switches and other network equipment.
  • Router and Firewall SetupConfiguration of MikroTik, Cisco and Fortinet routers and firewalls.
  • Wireless DeploymentsOffice Wi-Fi planned for coverage across your premises, with separate guest access where needed.
  • VLANs and SegmentationSeparating departments, guests and devices such as cameras onto their own network segments.
  • Monitoring and MaintenanceChecking network health and fixing problems as your needs change.

Our Approach

  1. Requirement analysis. We review your office layout, users and current equipment with you.
  2. Network design. We plan the cabling, equipment and segmentation before installation begins.
  3. Installation and configuration of cabling, switches, routers, firewalls and Wi-Fi.
  4. Testing and handover, followed by ongoing maintenance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you set up office networks in Dhaka?

Yes. Infyta designs and installs office networks for businesses in Dhaka, including cabling, switches, routers, firewalls and Wi-Fi. We can set up a network for a new office or extend an existing one.

Which network equipment brands do you work with?

Our engineers work with MikroTik, Cisco and Fortinet equipment for routing, switching and firewalls. The team includes engineers and instructors certified in CCNA, MTCNA, MTCRE and MTCSE.

Can you improve Wi-Fi coverage in our office?

Yes. Wireless deployment is part of our network infrastructure service. We review where coverage is weak, plan where access points should go and configure the wireless network, including separate guest access if you need it.

Can you fix or upgrade our existing network instead of replacing it?

Yes. We can review your current network, identify what is causing problems and recommend changes. Where the existing equipment is suitable, we reconfigure and extend it rather than replace it.

Do you maintain networks after installation?

Yes. Maintenance is part of Infyta's network infrastructure service. After installation, we can monitor the network, make configuration changes and troubleshoot problems as your business grows.
